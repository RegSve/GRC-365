Hihi everyone! My name is Regina Svejda, and I’m a graphic designer and illustrator based in Las Vegas. I’m currently attending UNLV as a Graphic Design major and a Marketing minor. I mostly make artwork for media I’m interested in, but I also produce work for my own characters and stories. I run a small business with my friends where we sell merchandise of our art and even attend conventions to sell at the artist alley.
